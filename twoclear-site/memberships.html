Looking after members' details

twoclear helps clubs look after member data and supports their data-protection duties. The club stays in charge of its members' information.

The right details, for the right people

The people running a session see emergency contacts and health notes for just that night's players, from the day before to the day after. Full records are for senior admins only, and an optional switch asks everyone who books, visitors included, for an emergency contact.

Logged, and erased on request

Every time a record is opened, edited, downloaded or erased, it is logged. The consent history can't be edited. Senior admins can erase a person's details when asked; their results and payment records stay so the accounts still add up.

The club's money, the club's account

Card payments go into the club's own Stripe account and straight to the club's bank. Card details never reach twoclear.
