Everything twoclear does

Your whole club, run from one place

No spreadsheet, no WhatsApp rota, no cash tin. twoclear handles the ratings, the court line-ups, the booking and the money — so your evening goes back to playing.

1 Ratings that mean something

A rating earned on court, not guessed

Every player has a rating worked out from actual match results — updated after each session. Nobody rates themselves, and nobody argues with the maths.

  • Ratings calculated from real results, refreshed after every night
  • Built on the proven Elo rating system, adapted for doubles — so beating stronger opponents counts for more
  • A public leaderboard on your club's own site — members see where they stand without asking anyone
  • Milestones and form indicators that surface the players who are improving
✓ Ends the "why am I on court 4?" conversation for good.
yourclub.twoclear.co.uk
2 Sessions that build themselves

The court planning becomes one click

Tell twoclear who's playing. It builds balanced courts and rounds for you — no more juggling names on a notepad while everyone waits.

  • Line-ups built from the ratings — by ability, mixed, mixed doubles, protecting newcomers, or random
  • Printable court slips for the night
  • Results go in by hand or by CSV upload
  • Social formats too: mixers, round-robin, king of the court, ladder, mexicano — scoring optional, just print and play
  • Split a mixer into named groups (different standards, or two halls) that never rotate into each other
✓ The pen-and-paper juggling that used to eat your evening becomes a single click.
yourclub.twoclear.co.uk
3 Recurring sessions

Set up a whole term in one go

Your regular club night shouldn't need creating over and over. Set it once and twoclear lays out the whole term for you.

  • Same night, same time, same settings — from a start date to an end date, weekly or fortnightly
  • Each night is then an ordinary, independent session — edit or cancel any one without touching the rest
  • Dates that already have a session are skipped, never overwritten
✓ Your Tuesday club night gets created once, not week after week.
yourclub.twoclear.co.uk
4 Booking & money

Members book and pay. You never touch cash.

Players book from their phone and pay by card — and the money lands in your club's own account. No app to install, no cash tin, no chasing.

  • Members book from their phone — no app store, no install
  • Club wallet: members top up by card and the money goes straight to your club's own Stripe account — you're never out of pocket
  • Capacity, waitlist, and automatic promotion when someone drops out — the promoted member and the host are both emailed
  • One session, two prices: members and non-members see the same session at their own price — no duplicate listings, and a newcomer sees the people already booked, not an empty list
  • Optional members-first window: members book first, everyone else after
  • Free taster sessions for newcomers, granted per person by an admin
  • Junior members carry guardian contact details, visible to the host only
  • Sessions can open for booking automatically, a set time ahead
✓ The cash tin and the "has everyone paid?" messages, gone.

Booking and payments are switched on for your club when you're ready — not before.

5 Running the club day to day

The admin that runs itself

The bits that quietly eat an organiser's time — sign-in, clashes, keeping the player list straight — handled.

  • No passwords. Admins sign in with a one-time code emailed to them
  • A combined calendar showing graded and social nights together — and it flags when two land on the same night, something a list can't show
  • Player admin with guards that stop you deleting someone whose results are already in the ratings
  • A CSV report of player activity, for spotting who's drifted away
  • Your club's branding, colours and logo on your own subdomain — yourclub.twoclear.co.uk
✓ Less time on the admin, more time actually playing.
yourclub.twoclear.co.uk
6 Memberships
New

Memberships, without the paper forms

Members join online, buy a membership on Stripe's secure page and renew by card each year on their own. Member prices apply themselves on every session.

  • Membership types with a yearly price and who each is for; joining part-way through the year priced per type
  • Cash or bank transfer recorded in a few taps, and it counts straight away
  • The renewal round on five tiles: Active, Card failing, Cancelling, Paid to club and Lapsed, with one-click "copy emails"
  • A treasurer's report for any period, downloadable as a spreadsheet
  • Emergency details for the people running the night, full records for senior admins only, and every look logged
✓ No January chasing, no cash tin, no spreadsheet of who has paid.
