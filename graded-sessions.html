Why run graded

A rating worth turning up for

Graded sessions turn a casual night into something your members genuinely care about.

Balanced matchups

RDR pairs strong with developing so every game is close — nobody's bored, nobody's outclassed.

Ratings that mean something

Every RDR is earned on your courts, from your results — not an app the player set up somewhere else.

Divisions with rating bands

Restrict a session to a rating range and run 3.0–4.0 and 4.0+ nights side by side.

HOT streaks & movers

Win streaks light up with a HOT badge and weekly rank changes give everyone a reason to check back.

Your rating, your club

RDR is independent of DUPR and owned by you — a number that belongs to your community.

Zero admin

No spreadsheets, no manual maths. Create, balance, score — the rating engine handles the rest.
