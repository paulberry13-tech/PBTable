RDR vs DUPR

Already use DUPR? Great. RDR does a different job.

DUPR is the global pickleball rating — a passport that travels with a player wherever they go. RDR is your club's own rating: earned on your courts, against the people your members actually play, week in, week out. One is about the wider world. The other is about your club night.

DUPRRDR (PBTable)
What it's forYour global skill ratingYour standing inside your club
Who needs itPlayers who travel or compete widelyEveryone in your club — no sign-up needed
Where it's earnedAnywhere, against anyoneYour sessions, your members
Who owns the dataDUPRYour club
Best atComparing across the worldRunning a brilliant club night

You don't have to choose. Most of our members have never logged a DUPR game — and RDR works for them from session one. If some of your players do have a DUPR, RDR sits happily alongside it. DUPR is your global passport. RDR is your home-court standing.

RDR is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a substitute for DUPR.
