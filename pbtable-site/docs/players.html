Players

Your roster: who is in the club, how to reach them, and whether they are a member. Everything about a player lives on one tab.

Who can use it

Club admins. Admin → Players.

Set up

Two ways to get started:

  1. Type them in, add a player with their name, and a starting rating if you have one.
  2. Import a spreadsheet, a CSV of name, initial_rating, gender, email. Gender and email are optional. This is the one-time way to seed a club with ratings you already hold.

What it does

  • Add a player, on their own or in bulk.
  • Edit an email address, gender and membership status inline.
  • Mark someone a member or non-member, this decides which price they see. See Prices and the wallet.
  • Mark someone a junior, with guardian contact details. See Newcomers.
  • Grant a free taster, if you have the offer switched on.
  • Bulk-import email addresses from a spreadsheet.
  • Create member logins, once booking is on.
  • Remove a player, with guards (below).

Removing a player

Nexorally checks before it lets you:

  • Refused if the player appears in any recorded match. Their results are part of everyone else's ratings, and deleting them would change other people's numbers. See How ratings work.
  • Warned if they hold a booking login, because the login would be left unattached.

Good to know

  • Ratings are not editable here, deliberately. A starting rating can be set when a player is first added; after that ratings come only from results. A hand-edit would fork the calculation.
  • Email addresses are never shown to members. They are visible to signed-in admins only.
  • A player and a booking login are separate things. A player is someone on your roster; a login is someone who can book. Linking them is what lets Nexorally know whether a booker is a member.
