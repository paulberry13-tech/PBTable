Graded sessions

A graded session is a club night that counts: the results feed every player's rating and the leaderboard. Nexorally picks who plays whom, builds the courts and rounds, and prints the slips.

Who can use it

Club admins plan and run graded sessions. Once published, the line-up is public, members can see who is playing without signing in.

Set up

  1. Admin → Graded → Plan next session.
  2. Pick the date, and give the session a name if you want one.
  3. Tick the players who are coming. The planner tells you how many courts that supports.
  4. Choose how courts are filled (below), then Generate schedule.
  5. Save plan, and publish it when you want members to see it.
  6. Print the court slips, play, then enter the results.

How courts are filled

Six ways, chosen per session:

  • Use my order, courts are filled straight down your list: the top four go on Court 1, and so on. It does not sort for you. Arrange the list yourself, or press Auto-seed to order it by rating first.
  • Balanced / mixed, players are distributed by rating so every court is an even, mixed-ability contest.
  • Mixed doubles, pairs are formed across genders where the numbers allow.
  • Protect newcomers, each court gets an experienced anchor, and new or unrated players are spread out rather than clustered together.
  • Random draw, a straight shuffle.
  • Keep pairs together, partners you have set stay together.

What it does

  • Works out how many courts your player count supports, and how many games each player gets.
  • Builds the rounds so people play with and against different partners.
  • Prints court slips for the night, and a session image you can share.
  • Takes results typed in on the night, or uploaded afterwards from a CSV.
  • Updates every affected player's rating as soon as results are saved.
  • Shows a "next session" page members can look at without signing in.

Good to know

  • One graded session per club per date. Choosing a date that already has one opens it for editing rather than starting a blank, the builder fills in with what is saved and shows a *Saved plan loaded* badge. If you want a second night on the same date, make it a social session.
  • Repeating runs skip dates that already have a session, they never overwrite them, and they tell you which ones they skipped. See Recurring sessions.
  • Publishing is separate from saving. A saved plan is yours to keep editing; publishing is what members see.
  • Ratings only move on graded sessions. Social nights never touch them.
