Set up a social night

Name it, pick your courts, set a price if you charge one. Leave Graded off so ratings stay untouched, then choose whether to record scores for a bit of friendly competition.

Recording scores is entirely optional and your call every night. Either way, nothing here feeds the RDR rating.

Why run social

The nights that grow a club

Social sessions are how new members find their feet, and how the regulars keep coming back.

Welcoming to all levels

Beginners and regulars play the same night, with balanced teams so nobody feels out of their depth.

Balanced, friendly games

The same team-balancing that powers graded nights, so every court is a good game, minus the stakes.

Record & share results

Tap in scores and share the night's winners with the club, or keep it purely casual. Entirely your call.

Simple sign-ups & waitlist

Members book from a link and the waitlist manages itself, no messages, no clipboard, no chasing.

One membership covers it

Social and graded run from the same club account. Switch a night from casual to competitive with one toggle.

Never touches ratings

Record scores or don't, either way, social nights never move anyone's RDR. Low-stakes by design.
