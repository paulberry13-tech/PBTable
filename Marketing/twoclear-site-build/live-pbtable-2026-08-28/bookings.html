Waitlist & refunds

Full sessions fill themselves

When a session is full the waitlist takes over. Cancel in time and it's a full refund to your wallet; cancel late and you're only refunded if a waitlister takes your seat — and PBTable promotes them automatically.

  • Automatic waitlist promotion
  • A fair, single 24-hour cancellation rule
  • No organiser chasing the group chat

Balanced court slips

Fair teams, generated in a tap

The moment your roster's set, PBTable builds the court line-ups in a tap — strong players paired with developing ones, and partners rotating every round. No captains huddled over a whiteboard.

  • Team averages within ~0.1 — every court a close game
  • Graded: ranking-based court assignments, so you play others at your level
  • Social: a choice of game modes — round robin, ladder, box league & coached nights
  • New & unrated players get a sensible starting level — never a faked rating — so first-timers still get fair, even games
