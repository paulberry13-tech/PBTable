Getting started

From spreadsheet to set up, in one sitting

There's nothing to install and nothing to build. You tell us about your club, we set it up, and you can run your first night the same evening.

1

Tell us about your club

Start your free trial and share a few basics — your club name, your colours, and how you like to run your nights. No card needed to start.

Not sure about something? We're a quick email away and happy to help you decide what fits.

2

We set you up

You get your own site on your own subdomain — yourclub.pbtable.com — with your branding, colours and logo, and your players loaded in.

Because every club runs on the same platform, setting yours up is a configuration, not a new build — so it's ready quickly, not in a few weeks.

3

Run your first night

Create a session, and PBTable builds the balanced courts for you. Print the slips and play. Pop the results in afterwards and your club leaderboard goes live for everyone to see.

When you're ready, we can switch on phone booking and card payments so members book and pay themselves — with the money going to your club's own account.

What you'll need

Honestly, not much:

  • Your club name
  • Your list of players
  • Your logo & colours (if you have them)
  • How you run your nights

That's it. No hardware, no IT person, no long onboarding project.
