## 4. The market and who we are selling to

Most are **volunteer-run**, often by a small committee, frequently by one or two people doing most of the work.

**Who decides:** usually the club chair, secretary, treasurer, or the one committee member who "does the admin". In larger clubs the decision goes to a committee meeting. ## 5. The problems clubs have today

These are the problems the product was built to solve. Use them as the emotional core of the campaign. They are grounded in how volunteer clubs actually run.

- **WhatsApp as the booking system.** A poll goes out, forty replies come back, someone counts them by hand. Nobody knows who's actually coming until they turn up. Newcomers can't see what's on.
- **Chasing money.** Cash tins, bank transfers to a volunteer's personal account, "I'll pay you next week", a treasurer reconciling screenshots.
- **The waiting list lives in someone's head.** When a player drops out at 5pm, someone has to message the next person, wait for a reply, then try the one after.
- **The same night listed twice**, once for members and once for visitors at a different price. It splits the roster, and a newcomer opening the visitor listing sees an empty session instead of the sixteen people already going.
- **Unbalanced courts.** Picking courts on the night by guesswork means strong players on one court and a newcomer out of their depth on another.
- **Ratings in a spreadsheet**, updated when someone has time, trusted by nobody, and a source of arguments.
- **Nobody knows where they stand**, so improving players have nothing to aim for.
- **Committee turnover.** Everything lives in one volunteer's spreadsheet, inbox or phone. When they step down, it goes with them. Shared passwords get passed around.
- **Juniors' details** are in a WhatsApp group or on a sheet anyone can open.
- **Who has quietly stopped coming?** Nobody can say until the numbers have already dropped.
- **Membership fees** collected by bank transfer once a year, with a spreadsheet of who paid, and no way to charge members less for sessions automatically.

## 6. The product — everything a club gets (live today)

Everything runs in a web browser on a phone, tablet or laptop. **There is nothing to install** and no app store. Members can add it to their home screen if they wish. Every club gets its own site at `yourclub.nexorally.com` with its own name, logo and colours, and everyone always has the latest version with nothing to update.

Tier codes: **[S]** Social · **[C]** Club · **[M]** Club + Membership. Higher tiers include everything below them.

### 6.1 The club's own site and public pages — [S]

- A branded club website: the club's name, logo and colours throughout, including the member booking app. Four colour themes, one of them dark.
- Public pages anyone can see **without an account**: what's on, published sessions, and who is playing.
- A club can restrict *who is playing* to members only. The count of how full a session is still shows, so a night never looks dead.
- Beyond a player's name, rating and results, nothing is public. No emails, no phone numbers.

### 6.2 Social sessions — [S]

For any club night that shouldn't affect ratings: mixers, club nights, beginners' evenings.
- **Five formats:** social mixer (everyone rotates partners and opponents), round-robin (everyone meets everyone), king of the court (winners up, losers down), ladder, and mexicano (pairings recalculated each round from how people are doing).
- The rounds and courts are built for you.
- **Scoring is optional.** Switch it off and it simply prints court slips for a relaxed night.
- **Court groups:** split a night into named groups that never rotate into each other — for running two standards at once, or two halls. Court numbers stay real ("Court 4").
- Several social sessions can share a date.
- Guests can be added for a one-off night, with a setting to hide guests' names from members.
- Printable court slips and a shareable session image for the club's WhatsApp or social media.
- An optional **social leaderboard**, kept completely separate from ratings, plus social participation stats (most sessions played, playing partners, new faces).

### 6.3 Graded sessions and the session planner — [C]

A graded session is a club night that counts towards ratings.
- Tick who is coming. The planner works out how many courts that supports and how many games each player gets, then builds the rounds so people play with and against different partners.
- **Six ways to fill courts**, chosen per night:
  - **Balanced / mixed** — ratings spread so every court is an even contest;
  - **Mixed doubles** — pairs formed across genders where numbers allow;
  - **Protect newcomers** — every court gets an experienced anchor, and new or unrated players are spread out rather than clustered;
  - **Use my order** — the organiser's own list, with one-tap auto-seeding by rating;
  - **Random draw**;
  - **Keep pairs together** — set partners stay together.
- Print court slips. Enter results on the night from a phone, or upload them afterwards.
- A "next session" page members can look at to see who's playing.
- A graded session can be named, and started from a previous one.

### 6.4 RDR ratings, the leaderboard and player profiles — [C]

- **RDR (Rally Doubles Rating)** — every player's rating comes from the games they have actually played. Nothing is self-assessed.
- Beating someone rated above you moves you more than beating someone below. An underdog who plays well can gain even in defeat.
- Every result is replayed in order from the full history, so the standings are always calculated, never nudged.
- **Ratings cannot be hand-edited.** This is deliberate and is a selling point: no committee favouritism, and no arguments about who fiddled the numbers. A starting rating is set once when a player joins, and a club's existing ratings can be carried over on day one so the table looks right immediately.
- **Confidence score:** each rating shows how reliable it is, rising with games played and fading with absence. Players are "provisional" until they have played five games.
- **The public leaderboard:** every player's rating and position, with filters to search by player or narrow by rating range, confidence level and games played.
- **Highlights after every session:** Most Improved, Biggest Mover, top risers (and optionally fallers), and the Top of the Club.
- **HOT streaks** 🔥 for players climbing several sessions in a row, **milestones** celebrated as players cross rating thresholds the club chooses, and a 👑 **top-player crown**.
- Players who haven't played yet are listed separately as "Yet to play" rather than ranked on a guessed number.
- **Player profiles:** rating, rank and top-percentage in the club, games played, wins and losses, win rate, peak rating and peak position, consistency, confidence, season movement, current streak, recent form, rating and position charts over time, and recent matches.
  - A player's first session shows how far they moved from their starting rating.
  - A player who missed the latest session sees the movement from their last session, with its date.
- Leaderboard images can be exported to share.
- **Every rule is the club's choice:** how long before a player counts as inactive, how long a streak must be, which milestones to celebrate, who gets the crown. Each can also be **switched off entirely**.

### 6.5 Planning tools — [S]

- **Recurring sessions:** set up a whole term in one go — every week or every two weeks until a chosen date. Each night is independent once created. Existing nights are never overwritten, and a whole run can be undone without touching anything people have booked.
- **The calendar:** graded and social sessions in one month view, with clashes highlighted.

### 6.6 Booking, payments and the wallet — every tier ([S], [C], [M])

Booking is optional and switched on for a club once its Stripe account is connected. A club can use everything else without it.

**For members** (the member app, at `/app` on the club's own site, branded as the club):
- Browse what's on, with prices, how full each session is and who is playing — **no account needed to look**.
- Sign in only at the point of booking, with a one-time code sent by email. No passwords.
- **Book in seconds.** The price and their balance are shown before they tap, so there are no surprises.
- **Join the waiting list** when a session is full, and see their position in the queue.
- **Cancel** and get the money back to their balance, within the club's rules.
- **Top up by card** into a wallet with the club, from preset amounts or any amount. The balance can only be spent at their club.
- See every payment in and out, and their upcoming bookings with a countdown to the next one.
- A player who belongs to two clubs on nexorally uses **one email for both**, with each club's sessions and wallet kept separate.

**For the club:**
- **Money goes straight to the club's own Stripe account.** nexorally never holds it, and it never passes through a volunteer's personal account.
- **Automatic waiting lists.** When someone cancels, the first person waiting is moved in instantly and emailed. The session host is emailed too, naming who dropped out and who came in.
- **When a session finishes,** anyone still waiting is automatically told the list has closed and that no money was taken.
- **Raising a session's capacity** admits people from the waiting list, after the screen tells you how many and asks first.
- **Scheduled opening:** sessions open for booking a set number of days ahead, automatically.
- **Session lock:** booking closes a set number of hours before the start, so courts aren't being re-planned on the night.
- **Two prices on one session:** a member price and a visitor price on a single listing, so each person sees the price they'll pay and the whole roster stays together.
- **Members book first:** optionally hold visitors back for a set number of hours after booking opens. A visitor who tries early is told *when* they can book, so they come back rather than give up.
- **Invite-only sessions:** choose exactly who can book a session (a squad night, a coaching group, a league match), optionally hide it from everyone else, and the invitees are emailed automatically — and again the moment booking opens.
- **Free sessions** (£0) sit alongside paid ones.
- **Free tasters:** give a newcomer a set number of free sessions, granted by an admin per person.
- **Comps:** give someone a free spot.
- **Book a member in yourself** and charge their wallet — for the player who rings instead of booking — even after the session has locked.
- **Juniors:** a player can be marked as a junior with a guardian's name and phone number, visible only to admins and the session's host, never to other members.
- **Self-signup** (optional): newcomers can join the club themselves by confirming their email address.
- Members are charged the price they were shown, and a session anyone has booked can't be deleted by accident — so every member is refunded properly.
- **A money report a treasurer can reconcile:** every top-up, booking, refund and adjustment, with a running balance and the session named on each line.

### 6.7 Memberships — [M] Club + Membership

Members pay their annual club membership online, and member pricing applies to them automatically. (Full detail in section 8.)

### 6.8 Running the club — [S]

- **Passwordless sign-in** for admins and members: a one-time code by email, typed in, never a link. Nothing to forget, reset or share. Committee turnover is simply a matter of whose email is listed.
- **Admin levels:** Senior Admin, Committee Admin and Club Ambassador. Senior Admins decide exactly what each level can do from a single permissions grid, and a new admin starts with the least access.
- **The roster:** every player in one place, imported from a spreadsheet in one go or added individually, with membership status, junior status and contact details kept admin-only.
- **Reports:** a downloadable attendance report — sessions played, when each player last came, membership status — so a club can see who has quietly stopped coming before numbers drop.
- **What's new:** release notes inside the admin area, listing only the changes relevant to the features that club uses.
- **Club settings:** branding, display rules, prices, booking defaults — all on one page, with explicit on/off switches.

## 8. Memberships in full — the Club + Membership plan

**What it is:** a club sets up its annual membership, members pay it online by card, and they automatically get member pricing on every session — without anyone updating a spreadsheet.

**How it works:**
- **One annual membership per club.** The club sets the price.
- **The membership year is the calendar year: 1 January to 31 December**, for everyone. That means one renewal date for the whole club, and no tracking of individual anniversaries.
- **Joining mid-year is pro-rata.** Someone joining in July pays for July to December, then renews on 1 January with everyone else. The calculation is automatic.
- **Members pay by card,** and it renews automatically each year. The money goes straight into the **club's own Stripe account**, as with booking.
- **Member pricing applies automatically** the moment someone's membership is active — on every session, alongside the club's other booking rules (members-first booking, two-tier pricing).
- **If a renewal payment fails,** the member keeps member pricing for **30 days' grace** while it is sorted out. After that they pay the visitor price until they renew.
- **Cancelling:** a member keeps their membership until 31 December and it simply doesn't renew. There are no refunds and no partial-year arguments.
- **An admin view of the whole membership:** who is active, who has lapsed, and who is cancelling, at a glance.
- **Cash and bank-transfer members still count.** Admins can mark anyone as a member by hand. An online membership can only ever *add* member status, never remove it — so nobody the club has deliberately marked as a member is ever repriced by accident.
- **Fee:** 1% of membership payments, plus Stripe's standard card fees.

**What membership buys, precisely:** member pricing. It does not currently include session allowances, priority booking beyond the club's existing members-first setting, family or household plans, or several membership types per club. **Do not claim any of those.**

**The benefit to sell:** the membership secretary stops chasing renewals and cross-checking payments. Members stop paying the wrong price. The club gets its membership money on time and in its own account, and one renewal date makes the year simple.

## 9. Trust, safety and fairness (what to say)

- **The club's money stays with the club.** It goes directly to the club's own Stripe account. nexorally never holds it.
- **Wallet credit can only be spent at that club.** It can't be transferred or withdrawn as cash. Refunds go back to the member's balance.

- **Safe by design.** A session that has bookings can't be deleted by accident, members are charged exactly the price they were shown, and a free session must be deliberately set to £0 (a blank price never means free).
- **Access fits the role.** Admin levels mean a Club Ambassador can help run nights without seeing the wallet, Stripe or member logins, unless a Senior Admin allows it.

## 16. Voice and style

- **First person, as me.** Plain, warm, direct British English. It should sound like a club member talking to another club's committee, not a company.

## 18. Glossary

- **nexorally** — the platform (formerly PBTable internally). Always lower case.
- **Club site** — each club's own branded site at `yourclub.nexorally.com`.
- **Member app** — the booking part of the club site, at `/app`. A web page, not an app-store app.
- **Graded session** — a club night that counts towards ratings (Club plan and above).
- **Social session** — a club night that never affects ratings.
- **RDR** — Rally Doubles Rating, a club's own results-based doubles rating.
- **Wallet** — a member's balance with their club, topped up by card and spent on sessions.
- **Two-tier pricing** — a member price and a visitor price on the same session.
- **Members-first** — non-members wait a set number of hours after booking opens.
- **Session lock** — booking closes a set number of hours before the start.
- **Invite-only session** — only chosen players can book it, and it can be hidden from everyone else.
- **Admin levels** — Senior Admin, Committee Admin, Club Ambassador.
- **Founding club** — one of the first 20 clubs on the Club plan, at £19 a month for life.
- **Discovery call** — the free call with me that every piece of the campaign points to.
