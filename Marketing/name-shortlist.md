# Name shortlist — round three, verified 23 September 2026

Brief, after narrowing: **an invented single word**, the Volleta family (which Paul liked), but with
a **firm ending** — "-eta" was too soft — and it must **speak to the club**, the people who turn up
together, not just the shot on court.

Three generators, each verifying its own candidates. **Every domain below re-verified by me**
against the registries (Verisign RDAP for .com, Nominet RDAP for .co.uk), each run calibrated first
against a known-registered name and a nonsense name. True on 23 September 2026; nothing is reserved.

## The five

| Name | Say it | .com | .co.uk | The idea | The symbol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Volleton** | VOL-uh-ton | free | free | Volley + **-ton**, the English suffix for the place people settle. The shot becomes a settlement — somewhere you belong. | A village-sign roundel, or a chevron badge |
| **Volquin** | VOL-kwin | free | free | Volley, landed on a hard *kw*. **Volkwin** is a real Germanic name meaning "folk-friend" — the etymology literally says *the people*. | Five dots in a V, or a V-notch badge |
| **Kithmont** | KITH-mont | free | free | *Kith*, as in kith and kin — the people who are yours by choice rather than blood. A club is your kith. | Two interlocking K-arcs |
| **Turnmont** | TURN-mont | free | free | Two turns at once: your turn on court, and the week coming round again. | An arrow bent into a circuit |
| **Vollorum** | VOL-or-um | free | free | Volley + *quorum* — the number who must turn up for play to happen. Exactly what the product manages. | A filled ring of counters |

## The strongest signal in the whole search

**Volleton was produced independently by all three generators, and ranked first by two of them.**
No other name across three rounds and thirteen reviewers has been arrived at twice, let alone three
times, by people who could not see each other's work.

## Honest weaknesses

- **Volleton** — a slight village-name tilt. VALETON (guitar pedals) exists: different vowel,
  different trade.
- **Volquin** — volkwin.com is registered (nobody trading on it), so a few people will write
  "Volkwin" when they hear it.
- **Kithmont** — "kith" is a word some buyers will not know unaided, so the story needs telling.
- **Turnmont** — the most literal and the least warm of the five.
- **Vollorum** — the "-um" ending is civic and slightly formal; nobody gets the quorum reference
  without being told.

## Also live, both domains free

**Volleden** (volley + den, warmer — but can be read "Voll-Eden") · **Rallden** (blunt, two
syllables) · **Shoalon** (a shoal turns as one body without anyone calling it) · **Clannix** (the
most instantly legible) · **Felagon** (Old Norse *félag*, the fellowship that gave English
"fellow") · **Perennon** (the same people back every season).

## Killed during this round, and why

- **Musteron / Musteran** — **Masteron** is the trade name of an anabolic steroid. Fatal for a
  sports brand.
- **Guildron** — strongest club meaning generated, domains free; **Guildron Trading Limited**
  appears in offshore-leaks coverage.
- **Ralliden** — one letter from Rallidae Therapeutics, a live 2025 trademark.
- **Rallmont** — one letter from **Valmont Industries** (NYSE).
- **Rondur** — one letter from **Rondor Music International** (UMG).
- **Volleron** — too near **Volero**, an actual volleyball club.
- **Sodalon** — both domains free, but two reviewers independently noted "soda" at the front.

Earlier rounds also killed: **Rallant** (Ralliant Corporation, $2bn NYSE), **Tandela**, **Plendo**
(Plend), **Volvera** (a live payments platform), **Tembrel** (Tembo Money), **Rallando** (Spanish
for "grating").

## Before you commit

1. Buy the .com, .co.uk and .app the same day you decide, before saying the name publicly.
2. Search the UK IPO register at trademarks.ipo.gov.uk — word, wildcard and phonetic/similar-mark
   tabs — in **classes 9, 41, 42 and 36** (36 covers card payments).
3. Say it down the phone and have someone spell it back.
